07

HOW THE DETECTION WORKS

STEP 01

Take Picture

The ESP32-CAM takes a picture of the plant.

STEP 02

Receive Image

Python receives the image from the camera.

STEP 03

Check Colours

OpenCV checks the colours in the image.

STEP 04

Save Result

The plant status is saved in data.json.

STEP 05

Show Dashboard

The website reads the data and displays the result.

STEP 06

AI Assistant

The AI can help explain the plant condition.
